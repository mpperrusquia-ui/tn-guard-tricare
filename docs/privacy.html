Privacy Policy — TN Guard Tricare Helper

Last updated: August 14, 2026

TN Guard Tricare Helper is an unofficial app that helps Tennessee National Guard soldiers and Airmen navigate TRICARE Reserve Select enrollment and the Tennessee Medical Readiness Act premium reimbursement program. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Defense, TRICARE, Humana Military, TriWest, or the Tennessee Department of Military.

What the app does with your data

The app does not collect, transmit, or have any server component at all. Everything you enter — checklist progress, and the information you type into the Enrollment, Attestation, and W-4 forms — is stored only on your device.

  • Sensitive fields (Social Security Number, bank routing and account numbers) are stored in the iOS Keychain, encrypted at rest and tied to your device.
  • Everything else (names, addresses, checklist state) is stored in a plain file inside the app's private sandbox on your device.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. The only way data leaves your device is if you personally choose to share a generated PDF — for example, using the share sheet to email a completed form to tntricare@tn.gov. That's an action you take, not something the app does on its own.

Content updates

On launch, the app checks a public, non-personalized JSON file (program links, premium amounts, deadlines) hosted on GitHub to keep that information current without requiring an app update. This request does not include any of your personal information — it's a plain file fetch, the same as loading a webpage.

Notifications

If you enable the monthly reimbursement reminder, it's a local notification scheduled entirely on your device. No push notification server is involved.

Third-party links

The app links out to official government and contractor sites (milConnect, tricare.mil, Humana Military, TriWest) using an in-app browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which this policy does not cover.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → "Clear All Saved Data" in the app at any time, or simply delete the app, to remove everything it has stored.

Contact

Questions about this app can be directed to the developer via the App Store listing. Questions about the TN Medical Readiness Act program itself should go to tntricare@tn.gov or your unit S1/personnel office.
